    Checking which signal supplies the dashboard…

    Sensor profile & CO₂ source

    Select the model printed on your module. SEN0219 v1 has an analog 0.4–2.0 V output; the newer PWM version needs different firmware. The two sensors are never averaged together.

    5.000 V is nominal. Use the measured Arduino supply voltage for a more accurate conversion. This adjusts the voltage calculation, not the sensor’s factory calibration. Changes apply to new readings; existing history keeps its recorded source.

    Why might CO₂ look lower than expected?

    160 counts is a voltage reading. At a nominal 5 V reference it is about 0.78 V. For the DFRobot SEN0219 v1 analog infrared module, the conversion is (millivolts − 400) × 3.125: approximately 1,191 ppm. The newer SEN0219 v2 uses PWM instead.

    Keep each sensor’s meaning intact. CCS811 estimates equivalent CO₂ from gas response, with a reporting floor of 400 ppm; it does not directly measure CO₂. Allow the manufacturer’s warm-up and initial burn-in. Missing DHT readings prevent fresh temperature/humidity compensation. The selected source supplies the dashboard; an unavailable primary sensor is never silently replaced by the other.

    History’s average is over time. Infrared CO₂ charts and averages include only samples recorded from that source. Older CCS811 and legacy mixed readings remain in exports with their source labels. Switching sources does not rewrite old readings.

    How the RAM buffer works

    Each buffered reading clears only after the Pi saves it to disk. Firmware v2 holds 24 minutes at one reading per minute. When full, the oldest record is replaced and the drop count increases. Power loss or a board reset erases RAM.

    WATER PROBE WIRING

    Three wires. One data pin.

    For a confirmed, externally powered DS18B20: VDD to the board’s 5 V supply, GND to GND, and DQ to D6 or D8. Use a separate 4.7 kΩ pull-up from each data line to 5 V. Do not infer wire functions from their colors.

    “Not detected” can mean missing power, the wrong pin, a missing pull-up, a damaged cable, or a different sensor model. Firmware identifies DS18B20 family 0x28 by its ROM and checks data CRC; it cannot identify an unknown three-wire probe by appearance.

    TWO OVERFLOW SENSORS

    Overflow sensor stops

    Name each sensor for its location, then choose which sensors stop each pump. Sensor 1 is Arduino D2; sensor 2 is D4. These assignments follow the pump function when you change relays.

    A selected sensor blocks starts and stops an active run on overflow, missing data, or stale data. “No overflow stop” disables that protection for the chosen pump. Emergency stop and the external safety switch still apply.

    Stop all outputs before saving. Changes leave schedules paused until you resume them.

    WATER PUMPS

    External safety switch

    Manual relay controls have no runtime caps, cooldowns, or operating-hour restrictions. This optional physical switch applies to all water pumps. Stop active outputs before changing its wiring settings.

    The switch input must be HIGH for pumps to run. A LOW or unavailable input blocks and stops pumping.
